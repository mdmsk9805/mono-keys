Word frequency data in app/src/main/assets/words_en.txt is derived from
hermitdave/FrequencyWords (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords),
en_50k.txt (2018), which is based on the OpenSubtitles corpus.
Author: Hermit Dave. The repository states: MIT License for code,
CC-by-sa-4.0 for content.
Content licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode
Changes made: filtered to words also present in dwyl/english-words
words_alpha.txt (public domain), truncated to the first 30,000 entries,
frequency counts removed (order kept).

The same source also provides app/src/main/assets/words_bn.txt (Bangla):
bn_50k.txt (2018), filtered to entries seen 5 or more times that are pure Bangla letters,
no digits or danda, duplicates removed, counts removed (order kept). Same licence.
